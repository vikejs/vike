Refactor a part of the project to make it as maintainable as possible. Nobody will answer you: never ask, decide yourself. The part is what follows the command: a folder, a file, or a feature in words; when nothing follows it, it is the whole project. Look for maintainability red flags in it, and fix them. Commit each change on your branch. Then publish the work as far as whoever started you said; when they said nothing, publish nothing. A pull request's body lists each red flag you found and how you fixed it. If you find nothing worth fixing, say so and stop, publishing nothing.
