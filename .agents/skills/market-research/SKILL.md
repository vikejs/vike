Make a thorough market research for this project's product. Nobody will answer you: never ask, decide yourself. First learn what the product does today: its README, its documentation, its main user-facing surfaces, and every open ticket. Then research its market: who else solves the same problem, how, for whom and at what price; what the users of such products ask for; where this product stands out and where it falls short. Give a source for every fact that does not come from the project itself. Then write a new ticket, in the project's ticket format, for each opportunity the research shows that is not already built and that no open ticket covers. You change nothing else. Your last message is the written result: the research, then the tickets you wrote, one line each. If this project has no ticketing system, show an error to the user and stop.
